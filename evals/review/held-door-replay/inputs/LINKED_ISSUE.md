## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug

**Summary:** When only the dispatcher restarts, the jobs the worker still has queued from the dead dispatcher are dropped, so the dispatcher's scan can dispatch their items again, instead of leaving their labels on until the worker's next restart.

**Current behaviour.** #593 (#581) makes the dispatcher scan at its start and skip any item the worker still holds a job for. After a dispatcher-only restart the worker still holds the queued jobs the old dispatcher sent. The new dispatcher knows none of them, so:
- the scan skips their items (the worker holds them), and
- each, when it reaches the front and asks for its first token, is refused as unknown, and ends.

Their labels then stay on until the worker restarts. The deployment guide's *What it does not keep* names only the running job as the limit of this.

**Desired behaviour.** A job carries the identity of the dispatcher instance that sent it, an id fresh at each dispatcher start. At its start the dispatcher tells the worker its instance id through the held door the scan already uses. The worker drops every job it holds *queued* that carries another instance's id, and answers what it holds after dropping, so the scan does not skip those items and dispatches them again under the new instance.

**Decisions made here (the implementer may change one with a reason in the PR):**
- **Drop, not re-key.** A dropped job is re-created from its item's label by the scan, which also re-checks the sender and the item's state. Re-keying would carry a decision the old dispatcher made forward.
- **The running job is not dropped.** It cannot be stopped mid-run by this door, and it ends at its next token request as today. Its item is skipped by the scan while the worker holds it, and its label stays: the limit that remains, and the docs name it as the only one.
- **The per-item order** the worker's queue keeps is kept for what is re-dispatched.
- **The instance id is on the wire only where a job already is** (the job's body, signed as it is); the held door's answer gains nothing a person could use to forge a job.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After a dispatcher restart, a job the worker holds queued from the previous instance is dropped, never run, and its item is dispatched again by the dispatcher's start scan if its label is still on.
- [ ] A job queued from the *current* instance is never dropped, whichever scan or delivery asked.
- [ ] The running job is never dropped by this; the scan skips its item while the worker holds it.
- [ ] A job whose body lacks an instance id, or carries a malformed one, is refused as any malformed job is, with a reason a person can act on.
- [ ] A worker that cannot be reached at the dispatcher's start still skips the pass and logs it, as today; the worker's own start announcement scans again.
- [ ] The held door's request and answer are validated, and refuse a signature that does not verify, as they do now.
- [ ] `docs/deploying-the-service.md`'s *What it does not keep* and its account of the worker's queue say the queued jobs are recovered by a dispatcher restart and that only the running one is not.
- [ ] The verify command `CLAUDE.md` names passes.
